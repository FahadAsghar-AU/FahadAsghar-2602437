Wah Cantt City

Introduction

Wah Cantonment (Wah Cantt) is a planned military and industrial city in the Rawalpindi District of Punjab, Pakistan, located about 30 kilometers northwest of Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Known as the "City of Education" due to its near-100% literacy rate, it sits adjacent to the historic town of Taxila and had a population of 400,733 in the 2023 census.
